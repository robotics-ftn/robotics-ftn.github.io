        The group studies how people and robots share tasks through nonverbal cues — where someone looks, how they reach, and what they are about to do next.

        Published models include a gaze dialogue model for nonverbal communication in human–human and human–robot interaction ([IEEE Trans. Cybernetics, 2024](/publications/gaze-dialogue-2024/)), action anticipation of human and robot intentions ([IEEE RA-L, 2018](/publications/action-anticipation-2018/)), and related RNN-based anticipation work ([ICRA, 2018](/publications/icra-anticipation-rnn-2018/)). Visual inference of object properties during handovers is developed further under [perception and tactile sensing](/research/perception-tactile-sensing/).
