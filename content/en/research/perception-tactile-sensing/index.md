        Perception work in the group combines what a robot can see during interaction with force sensing on the hand.

        From vision, the group showed that fullness of a container can be inferred from human visual cues during handover ([ICRA, 2022](/publications/icra-object-properties-2022/)). On the hardware side, the three-finger robot hand is force-sensitive ([ASME JMR, 2026](/publications/three-finger-hand-2026/)), and the earlier FTN hand used underactuated fingers with position–velocity sensing ([JIFS, 2018](/publications/ftn-robot-hand-2018/)). Gaze and anticipation during collaboration are covered under [human–robot interaction](/research/human-robot-interaction/).
