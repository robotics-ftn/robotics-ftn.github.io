        The group builds and teaches robot cells at the Faculty of Technical Sciences — ABB programming, gripper integration, and scaled factory automation that can be run in a teaching laboratory.

        Public work includes a scaled factory-automation cell with robot integration ([Applied Sciences, 2022](/publications/factory-automation-2022/)), Modbus RTU control of Robotiq grippers, and the open [open_abb](https://github.com/robotics-ftn/open_abb) interface. The same hardware underpins the Industrial Robotics course ([H308](https://ftn.uns.ac.rs/courses/H308/industrial-robotics/)).

        End-of-arm tooling on these cells is covered with the [dexterous hands and EOAT](/research/dexterous-hands-eoat/) theme.
