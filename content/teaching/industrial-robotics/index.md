**Industrijska robotika (17.H308)** — undergraduate course at FTN covering homogeneous transforms, DH notation, Jacobian, trajectory synthesis, robot dynamics, control, and programming.

- **Lecturers:** prof. Mirko Raković, vanr. prof. Milutin Nikolić (public FTN catalog)
- **Lab:** vanr. prof. Srđan Savić
